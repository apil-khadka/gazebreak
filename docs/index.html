Small reminders. Your rhythm.

Read the guide →

20-minute focus interval

The default is 20 minutes. Choose 20, 30, 45, or 60 minutes from the menu.

30-second break

A brief reminder to look into the distance. Choose a break of 20, 30, 45, or 60 seconds.

Menu-bar controls

Hover to see the countdown. Click for pause, resume, reset, and reminder settings.

Sound you can adjust

The 0.1.5 preview adds five completion sounds, a volume control, and a Test button.

Pauses around sleep

The focus timer pauses when the display sleeps or your macOS session becomes inactive.

Works locally

No account, analytics, or app network requests. Your preferences stay on your Mac.

Installation

Ready in two commands.

With Homebrew installed, add the tap and install GazeBreak. Then open it from Applications.

The tap currently serves Apple Silicon. Universal Intel and Apple Silicon builds are available as experimental previews. Check the release notes before downloading.

TerminalmacOS
brew tap apil-khadka/tap
brew install --cask gazebreak
