A quiet macOS utility

Make a little room for your eyes.

GazeBreak lives in your menu bar and gives you a gentle nudge to look beyond the screen—before a long session turns into a tired one.

Download for Mac ↓ Apple Silicon · macOS 13+

The rhythm

A small pause, at the right distance.

20minutes of focused work
30seconds looking away
15minutes for a longer reset after two hours

Quiet by default

There when you need it. Gone when you don’t.

GazeBreak stays out of the Dock and keeps its presence to a compact menu-bar pill. Pause it, reset it, or change the rhythm in one click.

01

Distance, not distraction

Each break asks you to soften focus and find something roughly 6 m / 20 ft away.

Respects your session

The timer pauses when your Mac sleeps or the session becomes inactive.
