01 / Get started

Ready in two commands.

Install Homebrew first if you do not already have it. Then add the GazeBreak tap and install the cask.

The current tap serves the stable Apple Silicon build. Intel support arrives with the stable universal cask. View releases for manual downloads.

TerminalmacOS
$brew tap apil-khadka/tap
$brew install --cask gazebreak
Then open GazeBreak from Applications.
02 / The rhythm

Small reminders. Better distance.

A practical cadence for close-focus work, kept visible enough to help and quiet enough to ignore.

03 / In the background

There when you need it. Gone when you don’t.

GazeBreak stays out of the Dock and keeps its presence to a compact menu-bar pill. Pause it, reset it, or change the rhythm in one click.

01

Distance, not distraction

Each break asks you to soften focus and find something roughly 6 m / 20 ft away.

02

Respects your session

The timer pauses when your Mac sleeps or the session becomes inactive.

03

Your rhythm, your call

Adjust intervals, break lengths, reminders, sound choice, and volume from the menu bar.
